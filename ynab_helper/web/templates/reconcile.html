When you'd want this

Reconcile is useful after a manual edit in YNAB or when the running total looks off. It reads the balance of the newest completed row of the statement and compares it with YNAB's cleared balance on that date. If they match, nothing happens. If not, it posts a single CSV Reconciliation adjustment (marked reconciled) so they agree, and you can investigate the difference at your leisure.
